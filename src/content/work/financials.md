Most medical sites hesitate to get involved in clinical research. Running a clinical trial at a site can be very time consuming. Most sites end up losing money because they aren't properly tracking the activities. When a site agrees to participate in research, they don't always budget properly - forgetting about the simple things like: _ice, gloves, paper_. In addition, they might not budget for things that are billable, resulting in a loss for the site.

### Problems

- Sites lose money because it's not always clear what should be included in a budget
- Sites are not always accounting for overhead, holdbacks, or inflation
- Seperate systems make it challenging to stay on top of the books
