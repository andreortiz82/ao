Most medical sites hesitate to get involved in clinical research due to the complexities of running a study and working with sponsors. In addition, sites might not invoice for things that are billable, resulting in a loss for the site.

At OneStudyTeam, we set out to improve this by building a financial solution on top of the [trial configuration services](/work/vwc/) - Ensuring sites can bill for every activity performed on every visit.

### Problems

- Sites lose money when participating in research
- Sites don't account for _overhead_ or _inflation_ when budgeting
- Sites account for protocol activities but don't account for site-specific activities

### Research

We spoke to site managers and others working in administration to better understand their financial process.

- Invoicing and Payments

When it came to the budget creation process, we wanted to start simple with basic line items due to our time and engineering constrains.
