Brand work is where I get to be the least practical version of myself — in the best way. No user flows, no edge cases, no stakeholder alignment meetings. Just: what does this thing *feel* like? What color is its personality? Does it have a logo that works on a hoodie?

I've worked on brands ranging from scrappy side projects to actual companies with actual budgets. Below is a collection of that work — some client, some personal, some just for the fun of seeing if I could. A few of these ended up on t-shirts. One ended up on a sticker sheet I still have a box of. That's the dream.
