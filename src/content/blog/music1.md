Music is the thing I do that has nothing to do with work, except for the part where it absolutely informs everything I do at work. Composition, timing, rhythm, contrast — these things translate. The patience required to sit with a chord progression until it resolves correctly is the same patience required to sit with a design problem until it actually makes sense.

In my spare time, I write, record, and occasionally publish whatever I've been working on. It's not always finished. It's not always polished. It's just whatever was interesting to me that week — a texture, a loop, a melody I couldn't shake. If any of it lands for you, great. If not, I still made it, which is the real point.

More on my [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@aomusicandmore) if you want the full archive.
