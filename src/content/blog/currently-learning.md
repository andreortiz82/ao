As the use of artificial intelligence (AI) continues to become more integrated into our daily lives, it's important to consider its impact on the user experience (UX). While AI is often focused on providing an experience in front of the screen, there are also many ways that it can improve UX behind the scenes.

When it comes to healthcare, one way that AI can help with UX is by improving the process of data entry. One of the biggest challenges facing healthcare professionals is the sheer volume of data they need to enter into any number of systems. In the world of clinical trials, for example, a healthcare worker could use up to 12 different software tools.
